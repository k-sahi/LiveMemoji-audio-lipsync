1. Waveform

2. Spectrum (512 FFT bins, 0–8 kHz shown)

3. Mel filterbank (26 triangles)

Each triangle sums the spectrum energy under it. They're narrow at low frequencies, where formants live, and wide at high ones, matching how human hearing works.

4. Mel spectrogram (26 bands over time)

Bottom = low pitch, top = high. Time scrolls right to left. Look for the horizontal stripes (formants) moving when you change vowels.

5. MFCCs (13 coefficients over time)

Bottom row = c0 (loudness), top = c12. Colored as z-scores: red = above that coefficient's recent average, blue = below.
