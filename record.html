    Recording tips
    • Use your laptop's built-in mic or a wired mic, not AirPods/Bluetooth (they delay the sound by up to 0.4 s).
    • Light on your face from the front (window or lamp behind your screen). Avoid strong backlight.
    • Sit about an arm's length away, face the camera, and keep your hands away from your mouth.
    • Speak at your normal volume. Vary pace and loudness a little; natural is better than perfect.
    • Keep this tab visible while recording (browsers slow down background tabs).
    • Do each take, then Download JSON before starting the next one. Mistakes are fine; just keep going.
